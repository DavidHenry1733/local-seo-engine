CRITICAL FIX: Stop Digital/Web Design Logic Leaking Into Non-Digital Service Pages

Current problem:
Generated pages for services like landscape gardener still read like digital marketing/web design pages. This is wrong.

For any non-digital business/service, the page must be written as if the customer business directly provides that real service to household customers.

Example:
If keyword = Landscape Gardener Sheffield
The page is for a landscape gardening business selling garden landscaping services to homeowners in Sheffield.

It is NOT about:
- web design
- SEO
- enquiries from websites
- AI search optimisation
- digital marketing
- business growth pages
- Google visibility
- customer engagement
- ROI from a website

==================================================
GLOBAL RULE
==================================================

If industryType is NOT:
- web-design
- local-seo
- web-hosting
- email-marketing
- digital-marketing

Then default buyerType = household.

The page must assume the business is selling directly to:
- homeowners
- householders
- landlords where relevant
- property owners
- families
- people improving their home

Do NOT default to businesses.

==================================================
SERVICE PAGE MEANING
==================================================

The keyword is the service being sold.

Example:
Landscape Gardener Sheffield means:
A landscape gardener providing garden landscaping services in Sheffield.

Plumber Rotherham means:
A plumber providing plumbing services to homeowners in Rotherham.

Roofer Barnsley means:
A roofer providing roofing services to homeowners in Barnsley.

Do not reinterpret the keyword as a digital marketing service.

==================================================

==================================================

For all non-digital services, block these phrases unless explicitly allowed:

- web design
- website
- SEO
- AI search
- Google rankings

If these appear in a non-digital service page, Final Publish Gate must mark REVIEW_REQUIRED or FAIL.

FINAL PUBLISH GATE
==================================================

Add NonDigitalServiceSemanticCheck.

For non-digital pages, fail/review if:
- digital/web/SEO terms appear
- common mistakes are website mistakes
- CTA is digital-service style
- content says business enquiries instead of homeowner service requests
- provider industry conflicts with campaign industry

Digital services sell visibility and enquiries.
Real-world services sell the actual job being done.

So for a landscape gardener, the page sells garden transformation, not “business growth”.
