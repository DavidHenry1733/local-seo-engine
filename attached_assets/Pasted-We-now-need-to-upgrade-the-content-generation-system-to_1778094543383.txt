We now need to upgrade the content generation system to produce high-quality, non-generic, locally relevant pages for any business type.

Do not change:
- layout
- image system
- section order

We are only improving the content prompt.

---

Goal:
Every page must feel like it was written specifically for:
- the business type
- the service
- the exact local area

---

Prompt requirements:

1. Structure (must follow exactly):

- H1 (service + area)
- Quick Answer block
- Local introduction
- Trust section
- Service explanation
- Local context section
- Problem section
- Service breakdown
- Process section
- Proof/trust section
- FAQ section
- Final CTA

---

2. Writing rules:

- Write in a natural, human tone
- Avoid repetitive phrases like “we offer” or “we provide”
- Vary sentence structure across sections
- Use short paragraphs (2–3 sentences max)
- Mix sentence lengths for readability

---

3. Local relevance:

Every page must include:
- nearby areas (2–4 locations)
- real-world local context (e.g. types of homes, businesses, demand patterns)
- realistic customer scenarios

Example:
“If you’re based in [area] or nearby places like [area1], [area2], you’ll likely…”

---

4. Problem-first writing:

Each service section should:
- start with a problem
- explain the impact
- show the solution

---

5. AI citation optimisation:

Include:

- “What Does [Service] in [Area] Do?” section
- “Quick Answer” (60–90 words, direct and factual)
- 2–3 mini Q&A style explanations within the page

---

6. Avoid generic content:

Do NOT generate:
- vague descriptions
- repeated phrasing across sections
- generic business statements

Each section must add new information.

---

7. Trust signals:

Include:
- experience
- reliability
- local knowledge
- outcomes (not fake stats)

---

8. Conversion:

Final CTA must:
- be clear
- reduce friction
- offer a next step (review, quote, audit, contact)

---

9. Content length:

Target:
- 900–1200 words per page
- Balanced across sections
- No single block too long

---

10. Variation engine:

Ensure:
- different opening lines across pages
- different phrasing for similar services
- no repeated paragraph structures

---

Goal output:
A high-quality local landing page that:
- feels written for the specific area
- is useful to real users
- is structured for SEO and AI extraction
- does not feel like a template or bulk-generated page
