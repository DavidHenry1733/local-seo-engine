The current output is still wrong.

That is completely incorrect.

Correct interpretation:
A “Landscape Gardener Sheffield” page is a public service page for a real landscape gardening business that sells landscaping work TO householders/homeowners in Sheffield.

The page owner/provider is the landscape gardening business.
The buyer is the householder/homeowner.

==================================================
ABSOLUTE RULE
==================================================

For every non-digital trade/local service page:

The page must be written in first-party service-provider logic:

“We provide [service] for [customer type] in [location].”

NOT:

“We help [service providers] get enquiries.”
“We create pages for [service providers].”
“We improve visibility for [service providers].”
“This service helps landscape gardeners.”

Example:
Keyword: Landscape Gardener Sheffield

Correct:
“Looking for a landscape gardener in Sheffield? We design and build practical, attractive gardens for homeowners, including patios, fencing, turfing, planting, drainage and full garden makeovers.”

Wrong:
“Landscape gardener Sheffield helps businesses generate more enquiries.”
“Landscape gardener Sheffield improves visibility for landscaping businesses.”
“DHM Digital helps landscape gardeners stand out online.”
