We need to fix AI image generation so it produces clearly relevant service-specific images.

Current issue:
Even with visual context, AI is still generating generic images (e.g. laptops, people working).

Root cause:
The prompt is too soft. It suggests visuals instead of enforcing them.

Fix:
Upgrade the prompt builder to use STRONG visual instructions and exclusions.

---

Update prompt builder rules:

1. Replace “Include visual elements such as” with:

“The image MUST clearly show the following key elements:”

2. Add exclusion rule to every prompt:

“Do NOT generate generic office scenes, laptops without context, or unrelated business imagery.”

---

Example for email marketing:

Replace weak prompt with:

"The image MUST clearly show:
- an email campaign dashboard with open rates, click rates or analytics
- a visible email interface or inbox showing marketing emails
- marketing performance data such as graphs or campaign results

Do NOT generate:
- generic laptops without visible email interface
- random office work scenes
- people sitting at desks without clear marketing visuals"

---

3. Enforce screen clarity:

Add:

“Any screen or device shown must clearly display relevant service-specific content (e.g. email campaigns, analytics, dashboards) and not generic blank screens.”

---

4. Apply this structure across ALL services:

- plumber → pipes, leaks, boilers MUST be visible
- electrician → fuse boards, wiring MUST be visible
- email marketing → dashboards, emails MUST be visible
- accountant → financial data MUST be visible

---

5. Final prompt structure:

wide angle composition, subject centred, designed for website layout with space around subject,

create a photorealistic modern commercial website image for a [businessType] business.

The image is for the [imageRole] section.

Show [roleScene].

The image MUST clearly show:
[service-specific visual anchors]

- generic scenes
- unrelated business visuals
- empty screens or vague imagery
