MASTER FIX — REAL LOCAL SERVICE PROVIDER COPY ENGINE

This is a critical production fix.

The generator must stop writing as if pages sell marketing services to tradespeople or businesses.

For all non-digital local service campaigns, generated pages must read as if they belong to the actual local business providing the service named in the keyword.

==================================================
1. CORE LAW
==================================================

The keyword defines the real service being sold.

Example:
“Boiler Repairs Leeds” =
A boiler repair business in Leeds providing boiler repair services to householders.

“Emergency Plumbing Rotherham” =
A plumber in Rotherham providing emergency plumbing services to householders.

“Landscape Gardener Sheffield” =
A landscape gardener in Sheffield providing garden landscaping services to householders.

“Roof Repairs Barnsley” =
A roofer in Barnsley providing roof repair services to householders/property owners.

The generator must never reinterpret a non-digital keyword as:
- a web design service
- a marketing service
- an SEO service
- a lead generation service
- a service for tradespeople
- a business growth service

==================================================
2. GENERATION ROLE LOCK
==================================================

Before writing any content, internally set:

pageOwner = “the local service provider”
reader = “the person who needs the service”
serviceSold = “the physical/local service specified by the keyword”
location = “the location specified by the keyword”

The page must be written from this perspective:

“We provide [service] in [location] for people who need [real problem solved].”

Never from this perspective:

“We help [trade/business] get enquiries.”
“We help [business type] rank locally.”
“We provide a digital solution for [trade].”

==================================================
3. DEFAULT AUDIENCE RULE
==================================================

For all non-digital local services, default buyerType must be:

household

This means write for:
- homeowners
- householders
- tenants
- landlords
- property owners
- families
- residents

Only use business/commercial audience if the campaign explicitly sets:

buyerType = business

Do not infer business audience from words like “service”, “provider”, “professional”, or “local”.

==================================================
4. DIGITAL LANGUAGE HARD BAN
==================================================

For non-digital services, ban these words/phrases from visible content, meta descriptions, schema descriptions, FAQs, cards, headings, AI summaries and trust strips:

web design
website design
website
SEO
local SEO
AI search
Google rankings
rankings
online visibility
digital marketing
digital presence
conversion-focused
lead generation
leads
search traffic
map pack

Google Business Profile integration

If any banned phrase appears in a non-digital service page:
- fail generation
- regenerate once
- if still present, mark REVIEW_REQUIRED and block publish

==================================================
5. SERVICE-LED KEYWORD LOCK
==================================================

If the user provides a keyword, the page must be built around that exact service intent.

Example keyword:
Boiler Repairs Leeds

The page must include:
- boiler repair
- boiler breakdowns
- heating issues
- no hot water
- unusual boiler noises
- leaks
- fault finding
- safe repair process
- callout/inspection
- local Leeds support

It must not include:
- web design
- rankings
- marketing
- business enquiries
- helping boiler engineers get leads

Every keyword must be parsed into:

primaryService
serviceModifier
location
buyerProblem
providerType

Example:
“Emergency Plumbing Rotherham”:
primaryService = plumbing
serviceModifier = emergency
location = Rotherham
buyerProblem = urgent leak/burst pipe/blocked toilet
providerType = local plumber

Do not guess with digital fallback copy.

==================================================
7. SECTION-BY-SECTION COPY RULES
==================================================

Every non-digital local service page must follow these rules.

--------------------------------------------------
META DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------

Must describe:
- service
- location
- household/customer problem
- practical outcome

Example:
“Boiler repairs Leeds for homeowners with heating faults, no hot water, leaks or boiler breakdowns. Book a local boiler repair visit.”

Never mention:
- rankings
- websites
- marketing
- business enquiries

--------------------------------------------------
HERO
--------------------------------------------------

Must say what the provider does for the resident.

Formula:
“Need [service] in [location]? We help [buyerType] with [real problems] using [real service deliverables].”

Example:
“Need boiler repairs in Leeds? We help homeowners with boiler breakdowns, no hot water, leaks and heating faults with practical local repair support.”

--------------------------------------------------
AI SUMMARY / QUICK ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------

Must define the real-world service.

Formula:
“[Service] in [location] means [providerType] helps [buyerType] with [real problems] by providing [real deliverables].”

Example:
“Emergency plumbing in Rotherham means a local plumber helps homeowners, tenants and landlords with urgent problems such as burst pipes, leaks, blocked toilets and overflowing water.”

==================================================
11. GENERATION SELF-CHECK
==================================================

Before returning content, answer internally:

1. Is the page clearly from the service provider?
2. Is the reader clearly the person needing the service?
3. Is every section about the physical/local service?
4. Are there zero digital marketing terms?
5. Are all cards, FAQs and CTAs service-specific?
6. Would a real homeowner believe this page belongs to a real local tradesperson?

If any answer is no:
Regenerate.
