- businessType
- primaryKeyword

- imageRole (heroImage, earlySupportImage, problemImage, trustImage, conversionImage)

heroImage:
A premium website-style image showing the main service in a clean UK setting.

earlySupportImage:
A service-in-action image showing tools, equipment or the process.

problemImage:
A realistic problem or issue the service solves, clean and professional.

trustImage:
A professional interaction or workmanship scene that builds trust.

conversionImage:
A finished result showing the outcome of the service.
