==================================================
FINAL SCORE STRUCTURE
==================================================

1. AI Structure & Extraction (0–20)
REPLACES:
- AI Ready

Purpose:
Evaluate whether the page is structured clearly for AI extraction and answer generation.

Checks:
- Quick Answer block exists
- extractable summaries
- heading hierarchy
- semantic structure
- FAQ quality
- concise answer sections
- clear informational formatting
- AI-readable content blocks

Scoring:
0–20

==================================================

2. Local Relevance (0–15)

Purpose:
Evaluate whether the content feels genuinely local and geographically relevant.

Checks:
- nearby areas referenced
- local context injected
- customer behaviour references
- area-specific language
- realistic local signals
- non-generic locality references

Scoring:
0–15

==================================================

3. Service Relevance (0–15)

Purpose:
Evaluate whether the page demonstrates genuine understanding of the service/business type.

Checks:
- service-aware content
- service-specific terminology
- dynamic service-specific mistakes section
- realistic business pain points
- service-specific CTA relevance
- contextual understanding

Examples:
Email marketing pages should discuss:
- segmentation
- subject lines
- engagement
NOT:
- page speed
- schema
- hosting issues

Scoring:
0–15

==================================================

4. Human Readability (0–10)

Purpose:
Evaluate readability and natural flow.

Checks:
- paragraph length
- sentence variation
- readability balance
- conversational tone
- non-repetitive phrasing
- scan-friendly formatting

Scoring:
0–10

==================================================

5. Internal Structure & Linking (0–10)
REPLACES:
- Structure

Purpose:
Evaluate structural completeness and internal relationship logic.

Checks:
- correct section order
- Related Services present
- Areas We Cover present
- internal links working
- CTA present
- image sections present
- FAQ section present
- no duplicate sections

Scoring:
0–10

==================================================

6. Conversion Quality (0–10)

Purpose:
Evaluate conversion and trust quality.

Checks:
- trust-building language
- CTA clarity
- reassurance
- enquiry-focused structure
- benefit-driven content
- clear next-step prompts

Scoring:
0–10

==================================================

7. Google SEO Fundamentals (0–10)
REPLACES:
- Google

Purpose:
Evaluate core technical SEO signals.

Checks:
- meta title
- meta description
- heading hierarchy
- schema presence
- alt tags
- internal links
- image optimisation
- section hierarchy

Scoring:
0–10

==================================================

8. Duplicate Risk & Variation (0–10)
REPLACES:
- Duplicate

Purpose:
Evaluate uniqueness and variation quality.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT only check exact text duplication.

Also check:
- semantic similarity
- repeated sentence patterns
- repeated openings
- repeated CTA wording
- repeated paragraph structure
- repeated section flow

Checks:
- exact duplication
- semantic duplication
- excessive repeated phrasing
- repeated templates
- low variation risk

Scoring:
0–10

HIGH SCORE:
Low duplication risk

LOW SCORE:
High duplication risk
