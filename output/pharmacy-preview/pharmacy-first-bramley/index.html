Is Pharmacy First available in Bramley?

Pharmacy First is available at PharmaConnect Demo Pharmacy, serving Bramley and nearby communities. Contact the pharmacy team or visit during opening hours to check current availability.

Do I need a GP appointment before Pharmacy First in Bramley?

For eligible minor illnesses under the NHS Pharmacy First pathway, you do not usually need a GP appointment first. A pharmacist assesses your symptoms and refers you to your GP, NHS 111, or emergency care where appropriate.

Can I walk in for Pharmacy First near Bramley?

Many patients can walk in for Pharmacy First at PharmaConnect Demo Pharmacy, subject to pharmacist availability. Call ahead to check waiting times or ask about booking options serving Bramley.

What conditions can Pharmacy First treat for Bramley residents?

Pharmacy First covers selected common conditions such as sore throat, earache, urinary tract infections (UTI), shingles, impetigo, and insect bites or stings, where you meet NHS eligibility criteria. Any supply of medicines is pathway-dependent — your pharmacist advises what is suitable for you.

Is Pharmacy First free for patients in Bramley?

NHS-funded where eligible; no charge at point of care for qualifying consultations.
