About Pharmacy Weight Loss Programme

Structured support combining lifestyle advice, monitoring and approved treatments.

Suitability & Eligibility

Who this programme helps

Adults seeking medically supported weight management

Individual assessment

Eligibility depends on BMI, medical history and current medicines. A pharmacist assesses whether the programme is safe and suitable for you.

Consultation & Assessment

Initial consultation

Your pharmacist discusses goals, lifestyle, previous attempts and any conditions that affect treatment options.

What to expect

Measurements and a structured conversation form the basis of your personalised plan — no guaranteed outcomes are promised.

Programme Structure

Phased support

Pharmacy Weight Loss Programme combines regular pharmacy reviews with lifestyle coaching and monitored progress over time.

GLP-1 and medicines

Where clinically appropriate and eligible, medicine options may be discussed under pharmacist supervision — individual assessment always applies.

Progress Monitoring

Regular reviews

Follow-up appointments track weight, wellbeing and any side effects to keep your plan safe and effective.

BMI and health markers

Monitoring helps adjust support over time and identify when GP referral is needed.

Lifestyle Support

Sustainable weight management includes nutrition, activity and behaviour support alongside pharmacy monitoring. Your team at PharmaConnect Demo Pharmacy focuses on long-term habits — not quick-fix promises.

Medicine Advertising & Safety

Important — weight management medicines

  • Weight loss medicines are not suitable for everyone and require individual clinical assessment.
  • Results vary — no guaranteed weight loss outcomes are promised.
  • Prescription-only options are not advertised to the public; suitability is assessed in consultation.
  • Report side effects promptly and follow pharmacist and GP guidance.
