What is Pharmacy First?

NHS-funded consultations for common conditions including earache, sore throat, UTIs, shingles, impetigo and insect bites without GP appointment.

Who it helps

Patients seeking NHS clinical care without a GP appointment

Busy working adults; Parents managing family health; Chronic condition managers

When to use this service

Use Pharmacy First for eligible minor illnesses when you need same-day pharmacist access without waiting for a GP appointment — where clinically appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Pharmacy First free on the NHS?

Pharmacy First is an NHS Advanced Service. Where you are eligible, there is no charge at the point of care for qualifying consultations. Eligibility and any treatment supplied depend on your individual assessment.

Which NHS service specification covers Pharmacy First?

Pharmacy First is delivered under the NHS Community Pharmacy Contractual Framework as an NHS Advanced Service. Specific pathways and eligibility may vary — your pharmacist explains what applies to your situation.

Where can I access Pharmacy First near me?

Pharmacy First is available at PharmaConnect Demo Pharmacy, serving Rotherham and nearby communities. Contact the pharmacy team or visit during opening hours to check current availability.

Can a pharmacy help with pharmacy for sore throat?
