Pharmacy First — questions for Reliable Direct Pharmacy

How do I book Pharmacy First at Reliable Direct Pharmacy?
Booking: Walk-in and telephone enquiries. Call 01142760150 to book or ask about appointment availability.
What should I bring to my pharmacy first at Reliable Direct Pharmacy?
Mention any medicines you take, allergies, symptoms and when they started. Call 01142760150 if you are unsure about preparation.
Can I ask the pharmacist at Reliable Direct Pharmacy to explain my pharmacy first options?
Yes — explaining your options in plain language is a core part of pharmacy first at Reliable Direct Pharmacy. The pharmacist discusses what the assessment means and sensible next steps.
What happens if I need GP or urgent care after speaking to Reliable Direct Pharmacy?
The pharmacist at Reliable Direct Pharmacy explains whether your symptoms need GP follow-up, NHS 111, urgent care or emergency care. You leave with clear guidance.
Can patients from areas near Ecclesall use Reliable Direct Pharmacy for pharmacy first?
Reliable Direct Pharmacy welcomes patients from Ecclesall and surrounding areas. Call 01142760150 for directions from your area and to confirm appointment times.
