Safety And Professional Standards — DHM Digital

Pharmacy checks screen for raised readings — they do not diagnose or treat hypertension on the spot. Diagnosis and long-term management remain with your GP. Pharmacist-led assessment. GP referral when indicated. Emergency symptoms need urgent care. Individual suitability confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Hillsborough pharmacy access — when should I see a GP instead?

Ongoing treatment, diagnosis, or symptoms such as chest pain require GP or urgent care — not screening alone.

Hillsborough patients — what symptoms need urgent care?

Chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe headache or sudden vision changes need urgent medical attention.

For Hillsborough residents: how often should I have my blood pressure checked?

Frequency depends on age, risk factors and previous readings. Over-40s and those with risk factors benefit from regular checks.
