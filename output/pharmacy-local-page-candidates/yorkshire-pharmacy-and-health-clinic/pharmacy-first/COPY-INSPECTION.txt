LOCAL FAQS
Q: What should I bring to a Pharmacy First consultation?
A: Bring a list of your medicines, note when symptoms started, and mention allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding. Call 01226 210477 if you are unsure whether to attend.

Q: How far is the pharmacy from Chapeltown?
A: Chapeltown is 9.8 km from the pharmacy. The pharmacy is at 91 Snape Hill Rd, Darfield, Barnsley S73 9LR, UK. Call 01226 210477 if you want to check the best way to get there.

Q: Should I call before travelling from Chapeltown?
A: Yes. Call 01226 210477 to check whether a consultation is available and whether your symptoms sound suitable for Pharmacy First.

Q: Where is the pharmacy located?
A: Yorkshire Pharmacy & Health Clinic is at 91 Snape Hill Rd, Darfield, Barnsley S73 9LR, UK. Call 01226 210477 if you want to confirm opening details before you visit.

Q: What happens if Pharmacy First is not suitable?
A: The pharmacist will explain when to use your GP, NHS 111 or urgent care instead, so you leave with a clear next step.
